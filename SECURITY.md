# Security Policy

This is a personal hobby project, maintained on a best-effort basis by one person.
It is not a Schuberg Philis or Lab271 product.

## Maturity

- The pages run entirely in your browser. There is no server, no account, and no
  data leaves your machine; scores and settings are kept in the browser's
  `localStorage`.
- There has been no independent security review of this code.
- Much of it was written by an AI agent under human review. The review is real,
  but it is not the same thing as an audit.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately. Do not open a public GitHub issue, and do not discuss the
vulnerability publicly until a fix is available.

Use the ["Report a vulnerability" button](../../security/advisories/new) under the
Security tab of this repository (GitHub private vulnerability reporting).

Please include:

- What the issue is, and what an attacker gets out of it.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The browser and platform you saw it on.
- Whether you want public credit or prefer to stay anonymous.

## What to expect

1. I aim to acknowledge your report within 7 days.
2. For confirmed issues, I'll agree a fix and disclosure timeline with you.
3. I'll credit you in the fix if you've told me you want public credit. By default
   you stay anonymous.

These are targets, not commitments.

## Scope

In scope: the code in this repository.

Out of scope: browsers themselves, issues that need a modified copy of the page,
and findings from a scanner with no demonstrated impact here.
